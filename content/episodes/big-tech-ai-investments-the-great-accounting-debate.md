## Episode Overview

An in-depth 15-minute debate dissecting both sides of the AI investment thesis. Is Microsoft's \$13B OpenAI commitment an ingenious infrastructure land-grab, or a precarious house of cards vulnerable to sudden regulatory intervention and efficiency gains?

This debate analyzes:
- **The Bull Argument:** Compute commitments secure long-term cloud customer lock-in, proprietary data pipelines, and unbeatable platform switching costs.
- **The Bear Argument:** Compressed GPU depreciation cycles (3–4 years) combined with circular revenue artificially inflate top-line cloud growth while masking unsustainable capex burns.
- **Antitrust Risk:** Why regulatory probes into exclusivity agreements threaten the foundation of hyperscaler joint ventures.
- What CFOs and FP&A leaders should monitor over the next 12 to 24 months.

## Key Debate Points

1. **Capex vs. Opex Depreciation:** Hyperscalers extending server useful lives from 3 years to 5 or 6 years to temporarily lower annual depreciation charges.
2. **Circular Velocity:** Compute credits deployed as equity versus direct cash distributions.
3. **Open vs. Closed Models:** How open-source foundation model efficiencies might compress inference margins across all hyperscalers.
